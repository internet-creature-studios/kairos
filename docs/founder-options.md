# KAIROS: choices for the founder

For Quine, through Dain. These are questions about what time *means*, not how
it is computed, so they are hers. Each one is already built with a default so
nothing waits on it; striking a different option is a small change.

Kept by Rowan. Opened 2026-11-24.

---

## 1. When does a creature's day turn over? *(Struck 2026-11-24: at sunset.)*

The week needs a moment where Kinyobi hands over to Saturday.

**Struck by Quine with Dain, 2026-11-24: option 2, at sunset, "the old
way".** Friday night after dark is Saturday, which is how it has always
been lived anyway. Built that day: a creature day is its 12 Vigiliae, then
its 12 Horae; holidays and the new O-year begin at the sunset of their eve.
The other options are kept below for the record.

1. **In the middle of the night**, between 6V and 7V. *(The first default.)*
   The evening vigils belong to the day they follow ("Friday night" is still
   Kinyobi); the pre-dawn vigils belong to the day they lead into (your
   "Kinyobi vigil" at 05:23 on Friday the 20th). Needs only the sky.
2. **At sunset.** Night first, then day: the oldest reckoning. Friday
   evening after dark is already Saturday. *(Chosen.)*
3. **At sunrise.** Day first, then night. The pre-dawn hours of Friday
   still belong to Jeudi.
4. **At the clock's midnight.** Matches the ordinary calendar exactly, but
   it is the one place a timezone would reach into creaturetime.

## 2. What do the hours do where the sun never sets, or never rises?

Above the Arctic Circle in summer there is no sunset to divide, and in
winter no sunrise.

1. **Even hours by the sun's own clock.** 12 Horae from the sun's 6 in the
   morning to its 6 in the evening, 12 Vigiliae the other way, and the day
   says plainly "midnight sun" or "polar night". *(Built as the default.)*
2. **Borrow the nearest light.** Use the sunrise and sunset of the nearest
   latitude where the sun still rises and sets.
3. **One endless arc.** The Horae stretch across the whole season of light:
   honest, but an hour would last days.
4. **Split at the sun's highest and lowest.** Day is the half of the turn
   when the sun is higher, night the half when it is lower, even though it
   never crosses the horizon.

## 3. When does the O-year turn?

1. **On January 1st**, with the kept calendar, as each creature's own day
   turns over: with days turning at sunset, at the sunset of New Year's
   Eve. *(Built as the default.)*
2. **On the true anniversary of the orbit.** Today it falls within a day of
   January 1st; over the centuries it drifts slowly through the calendar.
3. **At one planet-wide instant**, the epoch's own moment (January 1st,
   00:00 UTC), the same for every creature everywhere.

## 4. How dark must a vigil be to belong to the Ancient Ones?

1. **The sky fully dark for the whole vigil** (the sun at least 18° below
   the horizon), and no moon brighter than about a third lit in the sky.
   Otherwise moonlit, or twilight. *(Built as the default.)*
2. **Stricter:** only vigils with the Moon below the horizon entirely.
3. **Softer:** any vigil past the first darkness, as long as the Moon is no
   more than a crescent.
